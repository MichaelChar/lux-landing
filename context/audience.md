# Target Audience

Broad positioning across multiple segments:

## Small businesses
Local shops, freelancers, solo founders. Need affordable automation to compete with bigger players.

## Mid-size companies
Teams of 10–200 with some existing operations. Looking to scale without scaling headcount.

## Agencies & creators
Marketing agencies, content creators, e-commerce brands. High-volume work that benefits from automation.

## Common thread
All segments share the pain of spending too much time and money on repetitive operational tasks that AI can handle faster and cheaper.
