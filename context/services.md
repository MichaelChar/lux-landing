# Services

Services are grouped by verb-based categories. Each category has specific offerings underneath.

---

## Build
- Websites
- Apps

## Create
- Content generation (for businesses that produce content)
- Multilingual content (input content → output translated copies in multiple languages)
- Batch design generation (for A/B split testing, variation of details in designs)

## Market
- Market research (scraping & analyzing competitor pricing, features, reviews)
- Lead generation (scraping to find leads, automating personalized outreach)
- Customer reactivation (personalized messages to re-engage old customers)
- Ad management (distribute spend to best performers, cut losers, provide analytics)

## Support
- Email support automation (AI-powered email handling)
